# Modeler One — 10 Premium Logo Concepts

## Assumptions

- **Brand name:** Modeler One. **What it does:** an enterprise web platform that automates PBPK modeling and simulation, from question of interest to a signed ICH M15 assessment table and a reviewer-reproducible package.
- **Audience:** PBPK modelers, pharmacometricians, clinical pharmacology and regulatory-science leads at sponsors, CROs and agencies. **Personality:** calm, precise, rigorous, trustworthy, human. **Emotion:** confidence that comes from being checkable (relief).
- **Symbolic ideas:** the concentration–time curve (fast rise, slow decay), exposure (area under it), reproducibility, the signed record, "one" source of truth.
- **Colors:** Ink `#101B2A`, Amber `#F0A53A` (the warm shift in the film), Paper `#F6F3EC`. Avoid pharma-default blue and green. **Uses:** app icon, website, PDF reports and regulatory documents, the film's end card.
- Every curve is constructed from circular arcs and straight tangents, never freehand, so none of them reads as a swoosh.

---

### 1. Wordmark: "Rising Bars"
- **The idea:** the crossbar of every "e" in "Modeler One" tilts up at the same angle as the absorption phase of a PK curve. The letterforms carry the whole idea; there is no symbol.
- **Construction:** lowercase-leaning, x-height 52 % of cap height, stroke 1/9 of x-height (constant, no contrast), crossbars rotated 14° from horizontal on all three e's, "Modeler" and "One" separated by one e-width, 0.04 em tracking.
- **Typography:** custom geometric humanist sans, medium weight, open apertures, round terminals; a description for a type designer, not a licensed face.
- **Color:** Ink `#101B2A` on Paper `#F6F3EC`. One-color: Ink only; reversed: Paper on Ink.
- **Scalability:** the tilted crossbars survive at 16 px as slightly open e's; in black and white nothing is lost. Below 24 px switch to the concept 4 mark.
- **Motion:** the three crossbars draw in left to right, each rising to its angle on the beat of the film's tick.
- **Image prompt:** "Flat vector wordmark logo, the text 'Modeler One' set in a custom geometric humanist sans, medium weight, the crossbar of each letter e tilted upward 14 degrees, dark navy ink color #101B2A on a plain off-white background, centered, no mockup, no gradient, no 3D, no shadow, no extra text."

### 2. Lettermark: "Center Point"
- **The idea:** an "M" whose two diagonals meet at a solid dot instead of a vertex. The dot is the one record everything is rebuilt from (the compound parameter framework).
- **Construction:** two vertical stems (stroke 1/10 of height), two diagonals meeting at a circle of diameter 1.6 × stroke, placed on the baseline's optical center; corner radius 0 on the stems, round joins at the dot; letter width = 1.1 × height.
- **Typography:** none beyond the monogram; paired with a medium geometric sans.
- **Color:** Ink `#101B2A` letter, Amber `#F0A53A` dot. One-color: Ink with the dot knocked out as a hole.
- **Scalability:** at 16 px the dot merges with the diagonals and the form reads as a plain "M"; at 48 px the dot appears. In black and white the dot is a hole.
- **Motion:** the diagonals slide in from the stems and the dot lands last with the tick sound.
- **Image prompt:** "Flat vector monogram logo of the capital letter M built from two vertical stems and two diagonals that meet at a single solid circle at the center, navy #101B2A letter with the circle in amber #F0A53A, plain off-white background, centered, no mockup, no gradient, no 3D, no shadow, no extra text."

### 3. Abstract symbol: "Area Under"
- **The idea:** the area under a concentration curve, the measure of exposure, as one solid shape: a steep climb on the left, a long tail to the right, a flat base.
- **Construction:** flat base 100 units; left side is a quarter-circle of radius 28 rising to the peak at 28 % of the width; the right side is a tangent-continuous arc of radius 120 decaying to the base at the far right; peak height 56 units; no outline.
- **Typography:** none in the mark; the wordmark sits beside it in a geometric sans, regular weight.
- **Color:** Deep Teal `#0E3B43`. One-color: the same, solid.
- **Scalability:** a single filled shape; at 16 px it reads as a leaning hill, in black and white it is identical.
- **Motion:** the shape fills left to right as a plot would, then rests.
- **Image prompt:** "Flat vector logo, a single solid shape: a flat base, a steep rounded rise on the left, a long smoothly decaying curve to the right, dark teal #0E3B43, plain off-white background, centered, no mockup, no gradient, no 3D, no shadow, no extra text."

### 4. Negative-space mark: "One, Decaying" (recommended; SVG in `logo/concept-04-one-curve.svg`)
- **The idea:** a numeral 1 is cut out of a solid circle. Its flag is the decay phase of a bolus curve, read backward in time; the stem is the instant of dosing. One shape says "one" and "curve."
- **Construction:** circle radius 120 on a 256 grid; carved stroke 24 units, round caps and joins; stem at x = 144 from y = 70 to y = 188; flag a single cubic from (144, 70) to (82, 118).
- **Typography:** wordmark beside it in medium geometric sans, set at the circle's x-height; no type inside the mark.
- **Color:** Ink `#101B2A` circle, the "1" is true negative space (a mask), so it takes the background color. One-color: Ink on any light ground; reversed: Paper circle with the "1" showing the dark ground.
- **Scalability:** tested at 16 px and 256 px; the 1 stays legible at 16. Black and white is native.
- **Motion:** on the end card the carved "1" draws itself in one stroke, flag first, then the stem; the ink circle fills in behind it.
- **Image prompt:** "Flat vector logo, a solid dark navy #101B2A circle with a numeral 1 cut out of it as negative space, the top of the 1 curving down and to the left in a smooth decaying curve, rounded stroke ends, plain white background, centered, no mockup, no gradient, no 3D, no shadow, no extra text."

### 5. Single-line mark: "Steady State"
- **The idea:** one unbroken stroke rises and falls, rises again a little higher, and settles at a constant height: a dosing regimen reaching steady state. Patient accumulation, then calm.
- **Construction:** one path, stroke 1/12 of height, round caps; four arches of heights 40, 62, 74, 74 units on a 14-unit spacing; arches are circular arcs joined by straight descents; the path never crosses itself.
- **Typography:** wordmark below in regular geometric sans, 0.06 em tracking.
- **Color:** Ink `#101B2A` line on Paper. One-color: Ink.
- **Scalability:** at 16 px collapse to the first two arches; in black and white it is a line, so nothing is lost.
- **Motion:** the stroke draws left to right at the tick's pace and stops when the arches level.
- **Image prompt:** "Flat vector logo, one continuous line drawn without lifting the pen, four rounded arches that rise in height and then level off to the same height, uniform stroke, round line ends, dark navy #101B2A on a plain off-white background, centered, no mockup, no gradient, no 3D, no shadow, no extra text."

### 6. Modular / grid mark: "Seven Samples"
- **The idea:** seven identical dots sit on a seven-column grid at the heights of a concentration curve: the observed data. One dot, the peak, is amber.
- **Construction:** 7 circles, diameter 16 units, 28-unit column pitch; rows at 20-unit steps; vertical positions 1, 4, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 rows above the base; the peak dot (column 3) amber.
- **Typography:** wordmark to the right, regular geometric sans.
- **Color:** Ink `#101B2A` dots, Amber `#F0A53A` peak. One-color: all Ink, the peak dot enlarged by 25 %.
- **Scalability:** at 16 px use five dots; in black and white the enlarged peak keeps the idea.
- **Motion:** the dots appear one per tick, then a thin line connects them and fades.
- **Image prompt:** "Flat vector logo, seven identical round dots arranged on a regular grid so that their heights form a rise-and-decay curve, the highest dot in amber #F0A53A and the others dark navy #101B2A, plain off-white background, centered, no mockup, no gradient, no 3D, no shadow, no extra text."

### 7. Combination mark: "Agreement"
- **The idea:** two equal circles, one solid and one outlined, overlap by a small lens in amber: the observed and the re-run result, and the space where they agree.
- **Construction:** circle diameter 64, overlap 18 % of diameter, outline stroke 6; the lens is a separate amber fill; symbol height equals the wordmark's cap height × 1.6, with a gap of one e-width.
- **Typography:** "Modeler One" in medium geometric humanist sans, sentence case.
- **Color:** Ink `#101B2A` and Amber `#F0A53A` (two colors). One-color: Ink, lens left unfilled.
- **Scalability:** at 16 px drop the wordmark and keep the two circles; black and white keeps the lens as a gap.
- **Motion:** the outlined circle slides over the solid one until the lens appears, with one tick.
- **Image prompt:** "Flat vector logo lockup: on the left two equal circles overlapping slightly, one solid dark navy #101B2A and one outlined in navy, the overlapping lens filled amber #F0A53A; on the right the text 'Modeler One' in a medium geometric sans; plain off-white background, centered, no mockup, no gradient, no 3D, no shadow, no extra text."

### 8. Emblem / seal: "Timestamp"
- **The idea:** a ring of 36 equal ticks with one in amber: a signed, time-stamped record. It is the audit trail, not a decoration.
- **Construction:** ring radius 100, ticks 14 units long and 4 wide, 10° apart; the 0° tick (top) amber and 24 units long; a solid dot of radius 8 at the center; no inner text.
- **Typography:** none in the seal; wordmark below in regular geometric sans.
- **Color:** Ink `#101B2A` ticks, Amber `#F0A53A` signature tick. One-color: Ink with the signature tick longer only.
- **Scalability:** at 16 px reduce to 12 ticks; in black and white the longer tick carries the meaning.
- **Motion:** the ticks appear clockwise one per tick-beat; the amber one lands last.
- **Image prompt:** "Flat vector emblem logo, a circular ring made of 36 short evenly spaced radial tick marks in dark navy #101B2A, the top tick longer and in amber #F0A53A, a small solid dot at the center, plain off-white background, centered, no mockup, no gradient, no 3D, no shadow, no extra text."

### 9. Dynamic identity: "Their Own Curve"
- **The idea:** a fixed baseline with two anchor dots; the curve between them is generated from each project's own parameters, so every report, deck and signature page carries its own shape. Only the anchors never change.
- **Construction:** baseline 120 units, anchor dots diameter 10 at 0 % and 100 %; the curve is a one-compartment oral-absorption function drawn with stroke 6; peak position and height vary within fixed bounds (peak between 20 % and 45 % of width, height between 30 and 70 units); the static fallback uses 30 % and 56.
- **Typography:** wordmark in medium geometric sans beneath, never animated.
- **Color:** Ink `#101B2A` baseline and dots, Amber `#F0A53A` curve. One-color: all Ink.
- **Scalability:** static fallback at small sizes; in black and white the curve is Ink.
- **Motion:** the curve redraws between the anchors with each new project and stops on the tick.
- **Image prompt:** "Flat vector logo, a short horizontal baseline with a small solid dot at each end, and a smooth curve rising quickly and decaying slowly between the two dots, baseline and dots dark navy #101B2A, curve amber #F0A53A, plain off-white background, centered, no mockup, no gradient, no 3D, no shadow, no extra text."

### 10. App-icon glyph: "First Light" (recommended; SVG in `logo/concept-10-first-light.svg`)
- **The idea:** an amber half-disc over a horizon line: the morning after the overnight run, the peak seen as a rising sun, and the axis it sits on. It ties the logo to the film's cold-to-warm turn.
- **Construction:** 256 grid; rounded square, corner radius 58 (22.7 %); half-disc radius 56 with its flat edge at y = 152; horizon bar 152 × 14 at y = 164, fully rounded ends, 12 units clear of the disc.
- **Typography:** none in the icon.
- **Color:** Ink `#101B2A` tile, Amber `#F0A53A` disc and bar (two colors). One-color: Ink tile with the disc and bar knocked out.
- **Scalability:** tested at 16 px and 256 px; at 16 px it is a bright dome on a dark tile. Black and white is native.
- **Motion:** the disc rises behind the horizon bar over 1.2 seconds as the tile's background brightens.
- **Image prompt:** "Flat vector app icon, a dark navy #101B2A rounded square containing an amber #F0A53A half circle sitting above a thin horizontal amber bar with rounded ends, like a sunrise on a horizon, plain white background, centered, no mockup, no gradient, no 3D, no shadow, no extra text."

## Recommendation

1. **Concept 4, "One, Decaying":** it says "one" and "curve" in a single shape that can be sketched from memory, works at 16 px, and is true negative space, so it sits on any ground, including the film's ink end card.
2. **Concept 10, "First Light":** the strongest app-icon and favicon form; bold at 16 px, two colors, and it carries the film's warm turn into the product's daily surface.

Concepts 4 and 10 are provided as SVG in `logo/`. Concept 4 uses an SVG mask for the carved "1"; rebuild it as a boolean-subtracted path before sending it to print.
