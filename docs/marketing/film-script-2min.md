with the request. Single edit, exactly 2:00. The supplement at the end holds the extra deliverables the third master prompt asks for.

## 1. Assumptions

- Inputs were filled from the project: audience = PBPK modelers, clinical pharmacology and regulatory-science leads at sponsors, CROs and agencies; tone = calm, precise, regulatory-grade; brand mark for the end card = Concept 4 in `logo-concepts.md`; the web address is a placeholder (`[domain to confirm]`).
- The brief cannot be met on all three numbers at once: 200+ words, 40+ silent seconds, and a 120–130 wpm read need 96 s of speech for 200 words at 125 wpm, which leaves 24 s of silence. I held the word count and the silence, so the voice runs about 150 wpm inside short phrases (dialogue is faster). For a strict 125 wpm read, cut the lines at 0:47.9, 1:24.6 and 0:12.8 (32 words) and the count drops to 169.

## 2. Title and logline

**"Same Answer."** At midnight a modeler cannot reproduce her own figure; by morning a stranger can, and a four-year-old has an ordinary breakfast.

## 3. Creative concept

- **Human truth:** a dose is trust extended to someone who will never see the evidence. The modeler's private fear is being unverifiable; the reviewer's quiet duty is to check. The work is worth what a stranger can reproduce.
- **Motifs:** *visual* is the concentration curve (rises fast, falls slowly): a mismatched figure in Act 1, steam rising over a mug in the held shot, the logo drawing itself at the end. Cold tea has no steam at midnight; warm tea does by morning. *Sonic* is **the tick**: a wall clock at 60 bpm that becomes the score's pulse, then a key click, a pen cap, a pill-organizer lid and a syringe plunger on the beat, and finally one warm tick.
- **Emotional arc (RELIEF, never named):** exposed and alone at 11:38 p.m. → the plan signed before anything runs → a system that stops and asks instead of flattering her → a stranger reruns it and it holds → the proof is a boy who ignores his medicine and wants the blue spoon.

- **Signature:** the tick, 60 bpm, dry and close. Evolves clock → felt-piano pulse (0:41) → cello ostinato (0:44) → key and lid clicks on the beat (1:00–1:35) → silence (1:35) → one warm tick (1:42, 1:58).

- **Tempo and mood:** 60 bpm throughout, unhurried and exact, no drums, no risers, no swell hits.
- **Instrumentation:** felt piano, cello, a small string section, soft vibraphone only if the mix needs air. No synth pads, no vocals.
- **Diegetic sound:** laptop fan, refrigerator, wall clock, keys, mug on wood, rain on glass, pen cap, pill-organizer lid, syringe plunger, spoon on bowl. Record these hyper-close and dry.
- **Mix rule:** voice sits 3 dB above the music at all times; when the voice stops, the music drops to near silence rather than filling the gap.
