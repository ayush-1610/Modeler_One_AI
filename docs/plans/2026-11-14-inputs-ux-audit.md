# Inputs page: UX audit (2026-11-14)

Five lenses audited the Inputs page (the client-data review and the Delivery review) on two seeded projects:
`exampleamide-pbpk` (9 messy client files) and `clientamide-pbpk` (3 filled templates). Screenshots were taken at
1440x900 and 390x844 from the merged integration line. Findings are evidence for
`docs/plans/2026-11-14-inputs-task-first.md`; each names what the user sees and the root cause (file:line at commit
efa9fad).

97 findings: 15 blocker, 54 major, 28 minor.

## Structure and duplication

The Inputs page has no single structure. It stacks two intake pipelines, four file lists and four "still missing" lists on top of the five domains. On the delivery project (exampleamide-pbpk) the Delivery review is 5,542 px tall, 61% of it the "Study not stated" tables. It is mounted outside the tabs (InputsWorkspace.tsx:290-291), so on every tab the tab bar sits at y=6120 and tab content starts at y=6611, 7.3 viewports down. On Settings that content is 222 px of a 7,271 px page. On the template project (clientamide-pbpk) the tab bar sits at y=1705. Two review systems show the same studies and products: the legacy client-review cards and the Delivery subject cards. Their study lists differ (230-23-REF, 230-23-TEST and Nichols2012-IV appear only in legacy; 230-25 and 230-26 only in Delivery). Some Delivery cards say "shown here to read" but still carry blocker lists and dozens of "It is not data" forms. One file shows up in 5-6 places with conflicting states. One study shows up in 7-9 blocks, one concentration value in 3-4. The counters ("To confirm 12", the tab counts, "Still missing to run (19)") cover only the legacy pipeline. Empty states contradict the data shown next to them ("No studies yet" beside 6 datasets). A CSS conflict leaves a 520 px dead area on the sparse tabs. The Compound tab shows only the 6 S0-missing rows, so the user cannot fill any other parameter. Proposed target structure: a sticky left rail. Its first item is "Files received", which takes the place of the reading tray, the Delivery "What arrived", the files drawer and the per-tab file cards. It has one row per file (kind, read state, agent state, what it gave in both pipelines, the study or product it feeds). Each file expands to its sheets or tables, each with "how it is read" (approve the mapping), "whose it is" (assign) and "set aside". It also lists files with no study stated, with one Assign per file and the values collapsed. Then the five domains in PK-Sim order, each with an open-items count from both pipelines. The rail opens on the first domain with open work. Inside each domain: (a) "Open items" for that domain only, then (b) one card per study or product with exactly one confirm path (chosen by `confirm_on`), holding its facts, its observed profile or dissolution with NCA or Weibull fit, its datasets, its blockers and the sheets that feed it, then (c) the full parameter list by building block, with blank rows for anything not yet sourced. One "Add data" menu per domain. Collapsed by default: coverage numbers (shown as one status line), each file's regions and stage times, read-only template subject detail, the readiness checks (shown as a header popover), and the client request (one list with its download). Removed: the global Delivery block, the reading tray, the files drawer, the 260 px "Still missing" scroll box, the duplicate "Delivered, not in the data checklist" and "Add observed data" blocks, and the empty Solver card.

### [blocker] The Delivery review sits above the tab bar and repeats on every tab, pushing the tabs about 6,100 px down
- **Sees:** On every tab of the delivery project the page opens with the reading tray (119 px) and the Delivery review (5,542 px). The tab bar first appears in viewport 7 (y=6120, delivery-compound-vp07.png, delivery-studies-vp07.png, delivery-settings-vp07.png), and tab content starts at y=6611. The Settings tab's own content is 222 px of a 7,271 px page (delivery-settings-vp08.png): 76% of every tab is the same Delivery block. On a phone, 6 viewports (delivery-phone-studies-vp00..vp05) never reach a tab. On the template project the tabs start at y=1705 (template-studies-vp01.png). The 'To confirm' filter does not hide or filter this block.
- **Cause:** apps/web/components/inputs/InputsWorkspace.tsx:290-291 renders <ReadingTray/> and <DeliverySection/> as siblings before <div className="inputs-layout"> (:292), outside every TabPanel. This is the 'one slot beside ReadingTray' of docs/plans/2026-11-12-intake-contracts.md §8.10, which contradicts docs/plans/2026-11-03-inputs-and-client-data.md §3.2 ('no Client data domain: the client's data lands ... in the domain it belongs to'). Measured heights: delivery-coverage 117, delivery-files 901, delivery-mappings 496, delivery-unassigned 3390, delivery-subjects 436.
- **Fix:** Take DeliverySection out of the page top. Its file-level parts (What arrived, mappings, Study not stated, regions, Read again) move to a first rail item, 'Files received'. Each subject's part (blockers, values by table, spot check, signed confirmation) moves into that subject's card in its domain (Clinical studies, Products, Compound). The page top keeps only a one-line status ('9 files · 10 tables to approve · 6 files with no study · 12 to confirm'), each count linking to where it is settled. Record the deviation from §8.10 in the plan.

## Visual design and layout

The worst visual and layout problem is placement. InputsWorkspace mounts the legacy reading tray and the whole new Delivery review above the five tabs, so on every tab the user scrolls through the same 5,542-px block first: the tabs start at y=6120 on desktop and at y=11967 (about 14 screens) on a phone. A glass step bar that stays pinned on scroll then floats over that content and even over the tab bar, while the tab bar itself scrolls away.

Next comes noise:
- **Colour:** amber and cream mark about twelve different states. The Clinical studies tab shows 45 amber chips out of 128, and amber 'observed' is the same hex as 'waits on you'.
- **Repeated rows:** 10 identical 'waits for approval' rows, 10 'Study not stated' tables of up to 96 rows, and about 45 'It is not data' forms that make one card 8,114 px tall.
- **Repeated numbers:** the same counts appear three times as a header line, tiles and per-file sentences.
- **Repeated entities:** each study is shown up to six times in six forms.
- **Contradictions:** 'No studies yet' and 'Nothing on this tab yet' boxes sit directly under populated cards.

Then inconsistency:
- **Form controls:** native 19-px browser selects and inset pill inputs sit next to styled 34-px controls, because the only global control rule sets a border radius.
- **CSS collision:** the sidebar's `.brand` rule turns every `.chip.brand` into a 350×40-px blue bar in the sheet table.
- **Buttons and links:** 19 button styles, three link styles, 23 filled primary buttons on one tab, and 'Read again' in three sizes.
- **Headings:** card titles are grey and an h4 is larger than the h3 above it; one tab uses 15 font sizes.
- **Columns:** stacked tables do not share column positions.

Charts use half their width, shrink to 6-px labels on a phone, and swap the observed and fit colours between two plots of the same dissolution profile. Several existing kit components (DataTable, Clip, Skeleton, useTableView paging) are not used here.
